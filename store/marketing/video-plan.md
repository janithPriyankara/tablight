# Tablight short videos: plan

Goal: show people in different jobs that a pile of tabs is an everyday problem, that the browser gives them no easy
way to manage it, and that Tablight sorts it on their own computer in seconds.

## Format

- **Length:** 20 to 40 seconds each. One person, one problem, one payoff.
- **Shape:** 9:16 vertical for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. Each one is also cut at 16:9 for YouTube, LinkedIn and the
  Chrome Web Store promo video, which is a YouTube link.
- **Captions:** burned in. Most people watch with the sound off, so the story must read without audio.
- **Beat sheet, every video:**
  1. **Hook (0 to 3 s):** a tab strip of 60+ tiny tabs, with one line of text naming the person's pain.
  2. **The mess (3 to 10 s):** they hunt for one tab, click the wrong ones, and give up.
  3. **"There's no easy way" (10 to 14 s):** show the usual fixes failing: bookmarking everything, a second window,
     closing tabs they still need.
  4. **The Tablight moment (14 to 28 s):** open the dashboard with Alt+Shift+T or the side panel. Groups appear
     with names that fit their job. Show one feature that matters for this person.
  5. **Payoff and close (28 to 35 s):** the calm tab strip and the line "Runs on your computer. No servers, no
     tracking." End card: Tablight logo, "Free for Chrome and Edge", and the store name.

## Honesty rules (store policy, and our own promise)

- No accuracy percentages in videos. If a claim is needed, say "Anything it isn't sure about stays in Unsorted for
  you."
- Never show a real person's tabs, email, bank or work systems. Use a fresh demo browser profile with made-up tabs.
  Look-up answers come from the real Wikimedia sites, so only look up public names.
- Say "on your computer" and "no servers". Don't say "100% private" or "unhackable".
- The look-up is optional and sends one text you confirm. If a video shows it, it must show the confirm dialog.
- No competitor names or logos. No copyrighted music: use royalty-free tracks or none. Show film, show and game
  names only as tab titles, never their artwork.

- **"Your tabs never leave your computer" (privacy, 30 s).** Turn on airplane mode and Tablight still groups

## Production

1. **Demo profile:** a new Chrome profile with only Tablight installed. Open each persona's tabs from a list
   (a `.txt` of URLs) so takes can be re-shot the same way. Don't sign in.
2. **Capture:** 1080×1920 browser window for vertical (or crop from 2560×1440), 60 fps, cursor highlight on. Zoom
   in on the dashboard at the "Tablight moment".
3. **Edit:** CapCut, DaVinci Resolve or similar. Keep cuts under 2 s during "the mess", and hold 3 s or more on the
   payoff. Use a consistent caption style: Tablight's brand font and colour, placed above the platform UI safe zone.
4. **Voice-over (optional):** one sentence per beat, in the person's voice ("I'm a night-shift nurse…").
5. **Release order:** #9 (instant "wow"), #2 (Priority, new in 1.4), #3 (look-up), the privacy video, then one a
   week. Pin the privacy video on every profile.
6. **Measure:** store installs per week (store dashboard) and views/clicks per platform. Tablight has no analytics,
   so the store's own numbers are the only source; use a different link (UTM or short link) per platform.
