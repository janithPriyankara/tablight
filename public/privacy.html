Tab Organizer privacy statement

Tab Organizer is built so that your browsing stays on your device. In short: nothing leaves your device, except the optional sync of stashed tabs through your own browser account.

No network, no analytics

The extension makes no network requests. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Site icons are read from the browser's own icon cache, never downloaded from websites.

Grouping runs on your computer

Tabs are grouped by an AI model that ships inside the extension and runs on your device. It only uses each tab's title and address. Page contents are never read.

What is stored

The list of your tabs (address, title, group, open/sleeping/stashed state), your groups, stashed tabs, saved sessions, settings, the on-device model's numeric summaries (embeddings) of tab titles and addresses, and a few local backup copies of your stash are kept in the extension's local storage on your device. Private (incognito) windows are never recorded.

Sync of stashed tabs (optional)

If sync is on (the default), stashed tabs are synced through your browser's own extension sync storage, which is tied to your Microsoft account in Edge or your Google account in Chrome. Only the address, a short title, the group name and the date are synced, together with the record ids, timestamps and deletion markers needed to keep your devices in step. Open tabs and saved sessions are never synced. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings.

Export and import

Export saves a backup file to your computer; import reads a backup file you choose. These files are only ever written or read locally, by you.

Permissions

Tab Organizer reads your tabs to list and group them; uses storage to keep its data, with unlimited local storage so large tab lists and their embeddings fit; uses alarms for auto-sleep and hourly snapshots; uses an offscreen document to run the on-device model in the background; and uses the browser's icon cache for site icons. The optional "tab groups" permission is only requested if you turn on mirroring to the browser's tab groups.
