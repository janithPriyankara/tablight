Tablight privacy statement

Tablight is built so that your browsing stays on your device. In short: nothing leaves your device, except the optional sync of saved tabs and of the examples you teach your groups, through your own browser account.

No network, no analytics

The extension makes no network requests. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Site icons are read from the browser's own icon cache, never downloaded from websites.

Grouping runs on your computer

Tabs are grouped by an AI model that ships inside the extension and runs on your device. It only uses each tab's title and address. Page contents are never read.

What is stored

The list of your tabs (address, title, group, open, paused or saved-for-later state), your groups, saved tabs, saved sessions, settings, the on-device model's numeric summaries (embeddings) of tab titles and addresses, and a few local backup copies of your saved tabs are kept in the extension's local storage on your device. Private (incognito) windows are never recorded.

Sync of tabs saved for later (optional)

If sync is on (the default), saved tabs are synced through your browser's own extension sync storage, which is tied to your Microsoft account in Edge or your Google account in Chrome. Only the address, a short title, the group name and the date are synced, together with the record ids, timestamps and deletion markers needed to keep your devices in step.

With sync on, Tablight also syncs what you taught your groups, so your other devices group tabs the way you do: for each tab you moved into a group (or in a group you accepted), the text the on-device model read for it (its title, site and a few words from its address), the group's name and when you taught it. Up to 50 examples per group, newest first, within a small share of the sync space. Each device's own copy of the model learns them; they are only applied to a group with the same name there. Other open tabs and saved sessions are never synced. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Delete all data" deletes everything Tablight stored on this device (the tab list, groups, saved tabs, saved sessions, settings, embeddings and local backup copies of the saved tabs) and removes the synced copy of your saved tabs from your browser account's extension sync storage. It also turns sync off on this device, so another of your devices cannot sync the saved tabs back here. It does not close any tab. Uninstalling the extension removes the data stored on this device; to be sure the synced copy is removed as well, use "Delete all data" before uninstalling. Turning sync off stops syncing but does not remove the copy already in your browser account. Another of your devices that still has Tablight keeps its own saved tabs and can sync it again, so use "Delete all data" there too.

Export and import

Export saves a backup file to your computer. It contains your saved tabs, your groups and your saved sessions (the addresses and titles of the tabs in them). Import reads a backup file you choose. These files are only ever written or read locally, by you; keep them somewhere private.

Permissions

Tablight reads your tabs to list and group them; uses storage to keep its data, with unlimited local storage so large tab lists and their embeddings fit; uses alarms for auto-pause and hourly snapshots; uses an offscreen document to run the on-device model in the background; and uses the browser's icon cache for site icons. The optional "tab groups" permission is only requested if you turn on mirroring to the browser's tab groups.
