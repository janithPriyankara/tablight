About Tablight

Tablight keeps hundreds of open tabs under control: it sorts them by topic, finds duplicates, frees memory and remembers your windows, with an AI model that runs entirely on your computer.

Free for Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome.

What it does

  • Groups every tab by topic across all your windows: AI, Programming, Movies, TV Series, Shopping, News and more. Drag a tab to another group and Tablight learns from it.
  • Finds duplicates and closes the extra copies, with Undo.
  • Keeps a Priority group that only you fill: star a tab, or press Alt+P on it. It is listed first, and the model never puts tabs in it or takes them out.
  • Pauses tabs to free memory while they stay in your tab strip, or saves them for later in a reading list that syncs between your devices.
  • Remembers your windows every hour, so you can pick up where you left off the next morning.
  • Searches by words, with typos allowed, or by meaning; marks tabs you haven’t opened yet; and lives in a popup, a full dashboard or the browser’s side panel.

Private by design

The AI model ships inside the extension and runs on your device. Tablight makes no network requests unless you press “Look it up” on an Unsorted tab and confirm; then only the text you saw goes to Wikidata. It has no servers, accounts, analytics or ads, and never reads the content of the pages you visit, only each tab’s title and address. Read the privacy policy for the details.
