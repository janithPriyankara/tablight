Getting started

  • Open the dashboard with Alt+Shift+T, or click the Tablight icon and choose Open dashboard.
  • Open the side panel with Alt+Shift+S, or the Side panel button in the popup.
  • Add the tab you are on to Priority with Alt+P; Alt+Shift+P takes it out. On a Mac these are Option+P and Option+Shift+P. To change any shortcut, open chrome://extensions/shortcuts (in Edge, edge://extensions/shortcuts), or Settings → Keyboard shortcuts.
  • Take the guided tour with the ? button at the top of the dashboard, or Settings → Help.
  • Pin Tablight to your toolbar: click the puzzle-piece icon, then the pin next to Tablight.

Common questions

Why does the browser say Tablight can “read your browsing history”?

That is the browser’s wording for the “tabs” permission, which every tab organiser needs to see the titles and addresses of your open tabs. Tablight uses them on your device and never reads page contents. Nothing leaves your device except saved tabs and taught examples (synced through your browser account while sync is on) and, only when you click “Look it up” and confirm, the text you saw.

A tab is in the wrong group.

Drag it to the right group, or select it and use Move. Tablight learns from this, so similar tabs follow next time. Tabs that fit no group stay in Unsorted.

What is the Priority group?

A group only you fill. Star a tab in the dashboard or side panel, or press Alt+P on any web page, and it goes to Priority, which is listed first. The model never puts a tab there or takes one out, and learns nothing from what you put there. Auto-pause skips these tabs, and Close duplicates never closes a tab in Priority (or a pinned tab). Taking a tab out (the star again, or Alt+Shift+P) puts it back in the group it was in, on the device where you added it; if that group is gone, or the tab came from another device or a backup, Tablight sorts it like a new tab. The shortcuts do nothing on browser pages and in private windows.

My own group called “Priority” is now “Priority (yours)”.

“Priority” is now the name of the built-in group you fill yourself, so a group you already had with that name was renamed. It keeps its open tabs and what it learned; its saved-for-later tabs moved into Priority (they sync under that name, so your other devices keep them there too), and taking one out of Priority puts it back in your renamed group. Rename it again in the dashboard if you like (any name but “Priority”).

Alt+P or Alt+Shift+P does something else on a site.

Some sites use Alt with a letter as their own keys (for example, Alt+Shift+P is “Show preview” on wiki sites). While Tablight holds these keys, the site does not get them. To give them back, set other keys at chrome://extensions/shortcuts (in Edge, edge://extensions/shortcuts). On a Mac, Option+P otherwise types “π”.

What is the difference between Pause and Save for later?

Pause frees a tab’s memory while it stays in your tab strip; click it to resume. Save for later closes the tab and keeps it in your Saved for later list, which syncs to your other devices.

What does “New” mean on a tab?

It is a tab opened in the background that you haven’t switched to yet, for example a video you queued up. Use “Show new only” to see just those.

How do I move my data between Edge and Chrome?

Edge and Chrome don’t sync with each other. Use Settings → Export backup in one, then Import backup in the other.

How do I remove everything?

Settings → Delete all data, on each of your devices. Uninstalling also removes the data on that device.

Report a problem

Email us with what you did, what you expected and what happened, plus your browser and Tablight version (shown in Settings → About). Please don’t send lists of your tabs or backup files; a screenshot with private tabs hidden is plenty.
