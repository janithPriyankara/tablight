# Fine-tuning experiments (Task 4, 2026-11-20)

Base: BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5, fine-tuned with `scripts/finetune/finetune.py` on the Mac GPU (MPS), exported with
`scripts/finetune/export.py` (per-channel int8), head retrained with `CALIBRATION=<calibration.json>
node scripts/train-head.mjs` (threshold and similarity set only on the 20% calibration rows the encoder never saw),
then `HEAD_PATH=… MODEL_PATH=… npm run test:quality`. The sealed benchmark was not touched.

All variants use `--loss mnrl-seeds --batch-size 32 --calib-share 0.2`. `finetune.py` saves the epoch with the best
calibration-split kNN, so "ep 3/5" means epoch 3 of a 5-epoch schedule was kept.

## Run-to-run noise

mnrl-e5 was repeated with `--seed 2`. The seed sets both the training shuffle and the 80/20 calibration split, and
MPS runs are not bit-reproducible anyway, so this is noise for the recipe as a whole. Between the two runs:
holdout2 89.0 vs 88.6 (1 row of 255), sessions-tune 73.3/72.9 vs 74.8/74.1, sessions 72.1/72.7 vs 73.4/74.1,
holdout 90.7 vs 92.4, holdout3 91.6 vs 94.0, YouTube topics 82 vs 83 of 90, messy 84 vs 83 of 159, calibration kNN
90.8 vs 91.1, kept epoch 5 vs 3. So differences of about 1.5 points on sessions, 2 points on holdout/holdout3 and
1–2 YouTube videos are within noise. The variants e2/e3/e5/e5-lr1 are not separable on holdout2 (all 88.6–89.0).

## What is consistent

- Every fine-tune lifts calibration kNN (87.1 → 90.2–91.2) and sessions by +3.7 to +5.8 points in both scenarios,
  with fewer or equal wrong tabs except mnrl-e2 (18.4% / 17.2% wrong vs baseline 17.7% / 16.9%).
- holdout2 rises +0.8 to +2.3 points; only mnrl-e5 seed 1 (+2.3, 227/255) clears +2, and its repeat landed at
  +1.96 (226/255 vs 221/255), just under +2.
- YouTube topics is the blocker: five of six variants park 1–3 more topic videos in Videos and fail the 84/90 gate.
  The one that holds 84/90 (higher lr, mnrl-e3-lr5) gains least on holdout2 (+0.8).
- Messy YouTube is flat (82–87 of 159; gate 83). `other` tabs auto-assigned in sessions rise from 20/75 to 25–30/75.
- Speed and size are unchanged (354–358 ms vs 362–365 ms per 200; 34.0 MB). Parity ≥ 0.9988 mean everywhere.

## Verdict

**No variant passes the accept rule.** The rule needs ≥ +2 on holdout2 and on both sessions scenarios, no worse than
−1 on holdout, holdout3, YouTube topics and messy YouTube, all quality gates green, and ≤ 1.2× time.

- mnrl-e5 (seed 1): holdout2 +2.3, sessions +4.4/+4.4, but YouTube topics −2.2 (82/90) and the YouTube gate fails.
- mnrl-e5-s2: holdout2 +1.96 (226/255 vs 221/255), just under +2, sessions +5.7/+5.8, YouTube topics −1.1 (83/90), gate fails.
- mnrl-e3-lr5: all gates pass and sessions +4.2/+4.4, messy +2.5, but holdout2 only +0.8.

None of these recipes is shippable as is: "best recipe" below means the closest, not one that passes.

The best recipe on the choosing sets (holdout2, sessions-tune, calibration kNN) is mnrl-seeds, lr 2e-5, up to 5
epochs. It is the starting point for Task 5 (public data with `--extra`); its files are kept in the scratchpad as
`ft/mnrl-e5` and `ft/mnrl-e5-s2` (also `ft/mnrl-e3-lr5`, the one that keeps every gate). Command:

```sh
HF_HOME=$S/hf-cache $S/ft-venv/bin/python scripts/finetune/finetune.py --pool training/ --out $S/ft/mnrl-e5/model \
  --calib-share 0.2 --batch-size 32 --seed 1 --loss mnrl-seeds --epochs 5 --lr 2e-5
$S/ft-venv/bin/python scripts/finetune/export.py --model $S/ft/mnrl-e5/model \
  --out $S/ft/mnrl-e5/models/tablight/bge-small-tabs-v1
CALIBRATION=$S/ft/mnrl-e5/model/calibration.json MODEL_ID=tablight/bge-small-tabs-v1 MODEL_PATH=$S/ft/mnrl-e5/models \
  OUT=$S/ft/mnrl-e5/head.json node scripts/train-head.mjs
```
